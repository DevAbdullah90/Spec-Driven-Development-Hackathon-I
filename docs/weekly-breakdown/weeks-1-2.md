# Weeks 1-2: Introduction to Physical AI

This two-week block establishes the core conceptual foundation for the entire course, shifting the perspective from traditional digital AI to embodied intelligence.

## Topics Covered

*   **Foundations of Physical AI and Embodied Intelligence**: Defining the principles that govern AI systems operating in the physical world.
*   **From Digital AI to Physical Laws**: Understanding the challenges and requirements for robots to successfully interact with gravity, friction, and spatial constraints.
*   **Overview of Humanoid Robotics Landscape**: Surveying current commercial and research efforts in humanoid and bipedal robotics.
*   **Sensor Systems**: An introduction to the primary sensor modalities that feed data to the robot's AI brain:
    *   LIDAR (Light Detection and Ranging)
    *   Cameras (RGB and Depth)
    *   IMUs (Inertial Measurement Units)
    *   Force/Torque Sensors (for manipulation and balance)
