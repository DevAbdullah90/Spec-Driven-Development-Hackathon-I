## Why This Matters Now

We are witnessing a **paradigm shift**. Artificial Intelligence is leaving the server room and entering the real world. 

> "The future of AI is not just on screens, but in the physical world around us."

Humanoid robots are poised to excel in our human-centered world because they share our physical form. This represents a transition from **Digital AI** (Chatbots, Image Gen) to **Embodied Intelligence** (Robots that walk, grasp, and help).

:::info 🚀 **Course Mission**
To bridge the gap between high-level AI reasoning (LLMs) and low-level robotic control, enabling you to build agents that truly *understand* and *act* in the physical world.
:::
